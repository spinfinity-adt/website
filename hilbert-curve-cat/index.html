about
A Hilbert curve visits every cell of a 2ⁿ×2ⁿ grid exactly once, without crossing itself. Each segment here takes its colour from the cat photo underneath, so one continuous line ends up drawing the whole image.

Higher orders mean finer detail and far more segments — order 9 is 262,143 of them.
